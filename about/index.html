Founder and Director

Vijay K. Sharma, known as VS Sir

Ex-Navy Naval Artificer · AI-enabled educator · Author

VS Sir brings more than 20 years of professional experience. He began in the Indian Navy, then spent over a decade teaching NEET Physics at Kota's leading institutes, where he also served as Academic Director. Today he builds AI-enabled learning at VCA EduTech and AI.

  • 2002–2008 Naval Artificer, Indian Navy
  • 2012–2015 Physics Faculty (Medical), Aakash Institute
  • 2015–2021 Senior Physics Faculty (Medical), Allen Career Institute, Kota
  • 2021–2024 Academic Director and Senior Physics Faculty, Physics Wallah, Kota
  • Now Founder and Director, VCA EduTech and AI Pvt. Ltd.

Credentials: B.Tech Electronics & Instrumentation · CSIR-NET JRF, AIR 97 · GATE qualified (2012, 2013) · IIM Kozhikode eMDP.

Institute names appear only as employment history. VCA EduTech and AI Pvt. Ltd. is an independent company. Student results mentioned on this site were achieved by VS Sir while teaching at earlier institutes.
