VS Sir | AI-Enabled Physics Educator

Vijay K. Sharma (VS Sir)

Former Indian Navy Naval Artificer · Author · Founder and Director, VCA EduTech & AI Pvt. Ltd.

VS Sir brings more than 20 years of professional experience: from the Indian Navy to a decade of senior Physics teaching in Kota, academic leadership, and today AI-enabled education and automation work. He is the author of Physics and AI learning resources.

  • 2002–2008 Naval Artificer, Indian Navy
  • 2012–2015 Physics Faculty (Medical), Aakash Institute
  • 2015–2021 Senior Physics Faculty (Medical), Allen Career Institute, Kota
  • 2021–2024 Academic Director and Senior Physics Faculty, Physics Wallah, Kota
  • Now Founder and Director, VCA EduTech & AI Pvt. Ltd.

Qualifications: B.Tech Electronics & Instrumentation · CSIR-NET JRF, AIR 97 · GATE qualified (2012, 2013) · IIM Kozhikode eMDP.
