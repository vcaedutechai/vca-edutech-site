What are the fees?

Fees depend on the class, subject, tutor and number of classes per week. We share the exact fee after a short counselling call, so you only pay for what fits your child's needs.

Offline or online, which is better?

Home tuition suits younger students and those who need personal attention. Online works well when no tutor is available nearby or for senior students. We will advise you honestly.

Yes. Class 11–12, NEET UG and JEE preparation are guided by the same approach VS Sir has used in 20+ years of teaching Physics. We also offer the VCA CBT Test Series for exam-style practice.
