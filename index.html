School Integrated Program (SIP)

Turn your school into a NEET, IIT-JEE and Olympiad launchpad.

In towns without a coaching institute, the strongest students often have to leave for another city. SIP brings the whole programme into your own school instead. You pay a programme fee or consulting charge that we agree after a free assessment.

  1. Pre-FoundationEarly concepts, curiosity and study habits.
  2. FoundationCore Maths and Science, built for the road to JEE and NEET.
  3. NEET and IIT-JEEFull exam preparation with tests and analysis.

Olympiad training runs alongside every level.

  1. Free assessmentA visit or video call to understand your classes, students, staff and timetable.

  3. Launch and trainingClasses begin, and your own teachers are trained alongside our faculty.
  4. Monthly reviewTest results, attendance and progress are reviewed with the principal every month.
